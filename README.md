# BaseCity: a city built 24/7 by Blockies, the builders of Base

A live, watch-only voxel city tied to the `$BLOCKY` token on Base.

- **Every $10 = 1 Blocky.** Every $10 of `$BLOCKY` a wallet buys (added up per wallet) brings one Blocky to the city by blimp (`usdPerBlocky` in `src/config.js`; each buy counts at the price of its day, so changing it only affects later buys): Blocky #1, #2, ... with at most **10,000** in the city at once. Trading opens when $100,000 has been bought in total; from then a Blocky held a day is a citizen: an NFT its wallet claims (**BaseCity Blockies, BCB**, see below). Big buys arrive in batches. A single buy of $1,000+ also builds a **Whale Fountain** signed with the wallet, and jumps the build queue.
- **Newcomers, opening day, citizens.** Every Blocky starts as a newcomer: its wallet's `$BLOCKY` balance (tokens it had before count too) must cover it, so sell half of what it bought and the newest half of its newcomers leave (tokens moved away count as sold); freed places go to the next wallets in line (a waitlist once the city is full). **Opening day** is when `unlockUsd` ($100,000 = 10,000 × $10) has been bought in total (the HUD's "Bought"): from then a Blocky held `citizenDays` (1) is a citizen for good, whatever the wallet does, and an NFT it can claim. Held tokens cover newcomers first, so citizens free the rest. Both settings can be lowered any time (lowering `unlockUsd` opens trading at the next update), never raised after launch.
- **Basenames**: buyers show up by their primary Basename (name.base.eth) instead of their address: in the city log, toasts and news, Blocky cards, whale fountains, the collection (search by name too) and the claim page (look a wallet up by its Basename). `api/_names.js` reads the primary name from ENS's L2 reverse registrar on Base (ENSIP-19, where names are set since 2025), else the older Basenames resolver, and only keeps names that resolve back to the wallet (the name's resolver from the Base registry, its Base address record, else its plain one). Contract addresses are in `basenames` in `src/config.js`. Names are cached in KV (new wallets right away, known ones re-checked daily) and sent with `/api/colony` as `names`.
- **Base Avenue** (plots: `avenue` in `src/config.js`; projects: `src/projects.js`): numbered plots kept for Base projects' headquarters. A free plot shows "BASE AVENUE · PLOT n · Your project's HQ here" (and `adContact` / `xHandle`, which its sign links to). Add a project with one line in `src/projects.js` (the file explains every field): its `plot`, its goal `at` (Blockies), the day it was `added` (its HQ is never built before then, so adding one never changes what the city already built), colours, `style` (tower, campus, spire, dome) and `logo` (one drawn in code, `LOGOS` in `src/city.js`, or a square image in `public/logos/`). Until its goal its plot shows COMING SOON; then the crew builds the HQ like a landmark, and its signs link to the project. The gallery (`/gallery.html?only=hq`) shows every style. Landmarks are spaced out (`landmarkEvery`: at most one in every 3 projects), so homes keep going up even when many goals unlock at once.
- **Whale buildings** (`whaleTiers` in `src/config.js`): one buy of $1k+ builds a Whale Fountain, $2.5k+ a Whale Tower, $5k+ a Whale Skyscraper, ahead of everything else; towers go downtown (a free lot within 3 rings of Town Square, else in place of the oldest home, shop or office there). Each carries its whale's Basename (or address) on its crown, clickable to the wallet. The name stays while the wallet keeps at least half the Blockies it had right after the buy (`CitySim.whaleNames`, from departure times); sell more and the building goes dark, FOR SALE, and the next whale of its size or bigger takes it over (a new tower on that lot). The log, toasts and ticker say so.
- **Leaving leaves a mark** (`departures` in `src/config.js`, `CitySim.depart` in `src/sim.js`): Blockies that leave take the blocks they placed on the current construction site with them (the HUD and the log say how many), so the site takes longer. A big exit, `ruinAt` (20) or more Blockies at the same moment, leaves the newest home, shop or office abandoned (one per 20, at most `maxRuins`): grey, dark, boarded up and taped off, until the crew rebuilds it as the same kind of building, ahead of everything but whale fountains. Landmarks, parks and fountains are never touched. All of it follows from the departure times, so every visitor sees the same city.
- **Builds take time, on several sites at once**: the city opens one construction site per 40 Blockies it has had (at most 6, `sites` in `src/config.js`) and splits the crew between them. A crew's speed grows with the square root of its skill (4× the skill = 2× faster), so smaller crews on more sites build more in total, and bigger buildings take longer by tier (`buildTime` in `src/config.js`: ×3 small, ×4 mid, ×5 large, ×6 towers and skyscrapers; landmarks, fountains, land and the metro have their own). With ~100 Blockies a cottage takes ~9 min, apartments ~1.5 h, a skyscraper ~13 h. A new city gets a head start (`launchBoost`): its first project takes 8% of the time and each next one a bit more, up to the full time from the 80th, so with the 100 reserve Blockies the first buildings are up in minutes and ~50 in the first day. The HUD shows the time left, the next community goal and how many of the 10,000 Blockies are left. Land expansions and landmarks unlock at Blocky counts (10, 30, 75, … for land; 15 for Builder HQ, 100 for the airport, 1,000 for the metro, …). Up to ~120 Blockies walk the streets at once (the founder, the Base Builders, the first 20 OGs, every Legendary and the newest arrivals); all of them build.
- The city **starts from empty land**: grass, forest, a river. The founder builds the first garage, then the town square, then the city grows outward, roads appearing next to every new lot.
- ~30 building types by district: cottages, family houses, townhouses, apartments, villas, shops, cafés, offices, dev hubs, a school, GPU farms, towers, skyscrapers, wind turbines, water towers, farms, gardens, and leisure: a **roller coaster**, **Ferris wheel** and **carousel** (all animated), lake parks with ducks, flower gardens, playgrounds, skate parks, a public pool, soccer field, basketball court, concert stage and ice cream stand. Plus cars, buses and boats.
- **Base Builders**: real Base builders you add by hand build the city from day one with hand-made looks: the Founder, then Jesse (Builder 001), Nibel, Xen, Poet, Brian (CEO), Saumya Saxena, Jerry Pan, Ahaan Raizada, Jeremy Grinberg, Jon Roethke, Kien Nguyen, Toady Hawk, mleejr, deployer, David Tso, mrtdlgc, Quigley, DonJohnson, everythingempty, Cobie, statuette, Oxxbid and Joey. Add one line per builder to `legends` in `src/config.js` (`joined: 'YYYY-MM-DD'` to start later). `gallery.html?only=legends` shows them all, `?only=Jesse,Ahaan Raizada` just those.
- **Rare Blockies**: every Blocky rolls a rarity from its number and the block hash of the buy that brought it (`rollSeed` in `src/ledger.js`), so nobody can know or snipe a rare number before buying and anyone can check it afterwards: Common 70%, Uncommon 22% (Shades, Base Cap), Rare 7% (Gold Hard Hat, Laser Eyes, Astronaut), Legendary 1% (Diamond Skin, Crown). Rare ones walk the city, get a toast and fireworks when they arrive, and show on their card and PFP. Odds live in `rarity` in `src/config.js`; `gallery.html?only=rare` shows them.
- **Base Builders page** (`/builders.html`, linked from the HUD): every Base Builder's profile with a voxel PFP, title, live stats (hours building, blocks placed, share of the city, skill, building since) in a profile you can link to (`/builders.html#jesse`) and share on X, and a **Download PFP** button (1024×1024 PNG with a small BaseCity tag). Clicking any Blocky in the city shows its PFP too, so every buyer can download the Blocky their buy brought. Add `x` (handle) or `bg` (PFP colour) to a legend in `src/config.js`.
- Every other Blocky role has its own look: Founder (blue cap, gold badge), Smart Contract Dev (hard hat, hoodie, backpack), Frontend Dev (headphones), Designer (beret, scarf), Community (backwards cap, megaphone), Researcher (glasses, lab coat, clipboard).
- `gallery.html` shows every design and Blocky side by side; `?only=blockies` or `?only=coaster,ferris` to zoom in.
- **Watching live**: the city page shows how many people are watching now and how many have visited (`api/live.js`). Each open tab checks in every 2 minutes while it's visible; visitors are a hash of their IP in Redis HyperLogLogs (one per 2 minutes, one for all time), so no address is stored, a reload counts once and random ids can't inflate it. About one KV command per viewer per 2 minutes.
- **Blocky viewer** (`blocky.html`, `src/viewer.js`, at `/b/N`): any Blocky by its number, head to toe (or as its PFP or NFT art), with its rarity, trait, role, status, owner, arrival and blocks placed; ◀ ▶ (arrow keys) and Random; downloads: a 2048 px full-body PNG, the same without background, the PFP, a 1500×500 X header and the NFT art. Numbers not reached yet say how to bring the next one.

## The NFT: BaseCity Blockies (BCB)

`contracts/BaseCityBlockies.sol`: ERC-721 + ERC-2981 (5% royalty to the treasury) + ERC-4906, OpenZeppelin 5.

- **Token id = Blocky number.** The ledger (`src/ledger.js`, run by `api/colony.js`) decides which wallet brought which Blocky and when it becomes a citizen (`citizenAt`: not before opening day, `L.openedAt`). `api/claim.js` signs an EIP-712 claim for the wallet's citizens not claimed yet; the wallet sends `claim(ids, deadline, signature)` itself and pays the gas. Only the wallet named in the signature can use it; signatures last 30 minutes.
- **Only citizens are minted, so nothing is locked or burned.** A citizen never leaves the city, so the contract is a plain ERC-721: every Blocky trades on OpenSea from the moment it's claimed, no lock to lift later. Before opening day an NFT costs holding through it; after, holding `$10` of `$BLOCKY` for a day, which keeps day-traders from farming them.
- **Max 10,000** (`MAX_SUPPLY`, set at deploy, the same as the city's cap); the claim API never signs a claim the contract can't mint.
- **Team reserve**: Blockies #1 to #100 belong to the team wallet from day one (`nft.reserve` in `src/config.js`, or `TEAM_RESERVE_WALLET` / `TEAM_RESERVE_COUNT`): citizens on opening day like everyone's, never sent away by the hold rule, counted in the 10,000, disclosed on the About page and as `Origin: Team reserve` in their metadata. Set the count to 0 for none.
- **Metadata** from `api/nft/[id].js`: `/api/nft/206` (name, rarity, trait, role, status: Newcomer / Citizen / Left the city, citizen since, blocks placed, arrival date), `/api/nft/206.svg` (the portrait, drawn from the same 3D model by `src/voxel-svg.js`, cached for a year), `/api/nft/collection` (contractURI).
- Owner functions: `setSigner` (rotate the claim key if it ever leaks), `setBaseURI` (also asks marketplaces to refresh, ERC-4906: call it if the domain changes after deploy), `setContractURI`, `setRoyalty`.
- Build and test: `cd contracts && npm install && npm test` compiles (solc 0.8.26, Cancun), runs the contract tests on an in-memory Base and an end-to-end test of the claim and metadata APIs against it. `contracts/out/` holds the artifact (ABI + bytecode, used by `/deploy.html`) and the Standard JSON Input for Basescan verification.

## Run

```bash
npm install
cp .env.example .env.local
npm run dev                  # http://localhost:5173, also serves /api/colony, /api/claim, /api/live and /api/nft/* locally
                             # (installs any dependency a git pull added before it starts)
npm run build
```

Locally the API reads trades from GeckoTerminal like production; without KV the ledger lives in memory and starts empty. If the live API is unreachable, the pages fall back to demo data.

URL flags: `?demo` fakes 3 days of history and then a trade every 30s (a $1,000 whale at 2 minutes, a seller at 3.5 minutes); `?speed=600` fast-forwards the city clock for timelapse videos; `?weather=bull` forces the weather; `?photo` starts in photo mode. Combine them: `/?demo&speed=600&photo`.

## Go live (fresh city, 0 / 10,000)

1. **Storage**: add Upstash Redis from the Vercel Marketplace (sets `KV_REST_API_URL` / `KV_REST_API_TOKEN`). Required in production.
2. **Fresh start**: set `KV_KEY` to a new name (e.g. `basecity-v1`): a new key means a fresh ledger, so the city starts from empty land with only the team reserve (100 / 10,000, or 0 with no reserve) and only buys from now on count. Optionally set `LAUNCH_TIME_MS` (ms timestamp) to the launch moment. Do not set `BACKFILL_HOURS`.
3. **RPC**: set `BASE_RPC_URL` to your own Base RPC (Alchemy, QuickNode, …): the API reads every trade's receipt (the real wallet, and the block hash that seeds rarity) and checks holders' balances. A trade whose receipt can't be read yet waits, with every trade after it, for up to 10 minutes rather than count for the bundler; and one serverless instance at a time updates the ledger (a KV lock), so a Blocky number never goes to two wallets.
4. **Deploy and open the site** once so the ledger starts (`/api/colony`).
5. **Contract**: open `/deploy.html` with the dev wallet (`0x8eBA…C34D`), click *Generate a signer key*, copy the key, deploy. Then in Vercel set `NFT_CONTRACT` (the new address), `CLAIM_SIGNER_KEY` (the key) and `SITE_URL` (your domain), and redeploy: claims open. Claims stay closed on Vercel without KV (every instance must sign from the same ledger).
6. **Verify** on Basescan with the Standard JSON Input the deploy page links (compiler and constructor arguments are printed there too).
7. **OpenSea** lists the collection after the first claim; set its links and creator earnings there.
8. **Keep it ticking**: point a free uptime pinger (cron-job.org, UptimeRobot) at `/api/colony` every minute. The ledger only updates when the API runs, and the trade feed keeps just the last 300 trades of the pool, so a quiet night without visitors must not drop buys.

## How "24/7, same for every visitor" works

No game server. The crew's speed only changes when a builder arrives (`blocksPerHour × √(total skill)`), so total blocks placed is a piecewise-linear function of time. The city plan is a fixed, seeded sequence of buildings; each starts when the previous one is done and finishes once the crew has placed its cost in blocks. Any browser that knows every Blocky's arrival and departure (from `/api/colony` + KV) computes the exact same skyline, including when each building was completed.
