How it works

  1. 1 NFT = 1 vote. Every BaseCity Blockies NFT your wallet holds when you vote counts once. No NFT yet? Claim your Blockies.
  2. Holders decide. The team's Blockies (the reserve and team grants) don't vote.
  3. Free. You sign a message in your wallet: no transaction, no gas.
  4. Change your mind any time before the round ends: vote again. A vote follows its NFT, so nothing counts twice.
  5. The winner gets built. When the round ends, the winning choice goes into the city plan and the crew breaks ground.
