How it works

  1. Buy, get Blockies. Every $%PRICE% of $BLOCKY a wallet buys, added up over all its buys, brings one Blocky to the city, numbered in order. At most 10,000 live in the city.
  2. Hold %DAYS% days. A new Blocky is a newcomer: sell $BLOCKY and newcomers leave the city, newest first, their places going to the next buyers.
  3. Become a citizen. After %DAYS% days in the city a Blocky is a citizen for good, whatever you do with your $BLOCKY.
  4. Claim and trade. Connect that wallet and claim your citizens: they're minted to it as BaseCity Blockies (BCB) NFTs, free to trade on OpenSea at once. You pay the gas. 5% royalty.
