How it works

  1. Buy, get Blockies. Every $%PRICE% of $BLOCKY a wallet buys, added up over all its buys, brings one Blocky to the city, numbered in order. At most 10,000 live in the city.
  2. Claim. Connect that wallet and claim: the Blockies are minted to it as BaseCity Blockies (BCB) NFTs. You pay the gas.
  3. Hold to stay. Sell $BLOCKY and the same share of your Blockies leaves the city, newest first, even if claimed. Their places go to the next buyers.
  4. Locked until 10,000. Blockies can't be traded until all of them are claimed. Then the collection opens on OpenSea and every Blocky stays for good. 5% royalty.
