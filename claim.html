How it works

  1. Buy, get Blockies. Every $%PRICE% of $BLOCKY a wallet buys, added up over all its buys, brings one Blocky to the city, numbered in order. At most 10,000 live in the city.
  2. Hold %HELD%. A new Blocky is a newcomer: sell $BLOCKY and newcomers leave the city, newest first, their places going to the next buyers.
  3. Citizen for good. A Blocky held %HELD% is a citizen: it never leaves, whatever you do with your $BLOCKY afterwards.
  4. Claim and trade. Connect that wallet and claim your citizens: they're minted to it as BaseCity Blockies (BCB) NFTs, free to trade on OpenSea at once. You pay the gas. 5% royalty. Only 10,000 can ever be minted: when they're gone, the collection is complete.

List on OpenSea

Your wallet approves OpenSea for the collection once (a transaction, a few cents), then one signature lists them, no gas (a smart wallet, like Coinbase Smart Wallet, signs each listing on its own). Prices are in ETH; OpenSea's fee and the 5% creator earnings come out of the sale.
